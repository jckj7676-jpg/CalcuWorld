How CalcuWorld Helps You

💼 Plan Finances with Confidence

Use our mortgage, personal loan, compound interest and sales tax calculators to compare offers, estimate monthly costs and understand how interest compounds. Every result updates live as you type — no page reloads, no waiting.

❤️ Track Health & Fitness Metrics

Our BMI, TDEE and body fat calculators use widely accepted formulas — the Mifflin–St Jeor equation for daily energy needs and the U.S. Navy circumference method for body fat. Use them for general wellness awareness, not medical diagnosis.

🧮 Solve Math Instantly

From trig and logarithms to unit conversion between length, weight and temperature, CalcuWorld's math tools work offline in your browser. Input validation prevents divide-by-zero and other common errors.

📅 Handle Everyday Numbers

Calculate your age in years, months and days, find the exact number of days between two dates, or split a restaurant bill with a custom tip — all in one place, with instant results.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are CalcuWorld calculators free to use?

Yes. Every calculator on CalcuWorld is 100% free, requires no sign-up and runs entirely in your browser.

Is my input data private?

Yes. All calculations happen locally in your browser using JavaScript. Your numbers are never transmitted to any server.

How accurate are the health calculators?

BMI, TDEE and body fat calculators use standard formulas (Mifflin–St Jeor, U.S. Navy method). They provide helpful estimates but are not a substitute for professional medical advice.

Can I use CalcuWorld on mobile?

Absolutely. CalcuWorld is fully responsive and works on phones, tablets and desktops. Layouts adapt to your screen size automatically.

Do you store any cookies?

We use localStorage only to remember your theme (dark/light) and language choice. Advertising partners may set cookies — see our Privacy Policy and Cookie Policy for details.

How often are calculators updated?

We review formulas and usability regularly. If you spot an issue, reach out via the Contact page — we appreciate feedback.
