About CalcuWorld

CalcuWorld provides free online calculators for common financial, mathematical, health-related and everyday calculations.

Our approach

We aim to make calculations understandable and easy to use. Calculator results are generated in your browser. Each tool includes information about its inputs, units or calculation method where applicable.

Accuracy and limitations

We work to test and improve our calculators, but results may be estimates and should be checked before being used for financial, health, legal or other important decisions. Please report unexpected results through our contact page.
